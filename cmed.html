Operating Modes

Four precision modes

The C-MED offers three core operating modes for different techniques, plus a dedicated P-Shot mode for collagen bio-induction procedures.

M1
Regular
Continuous, consistent needle action. Ideal for fill work, shading and most standard PMU techniques.
M2
Intermittent
Pulse-based delivery for sensitive areas. Reduces trauma on delicate skin.
M3
Gradual
Progressive build-up in each pass. Precise control for hair strokes and fine detail work.
P-Shot
P-Shot
Dedicated mode for collagen bio-induction treatments.
Setup & Use

Ready in three steps

1

Attach the needle cartridge

Compatible with universal needles and Fly by Clinita cartridges. Bayonet-style connection clicks securely into place.

2

Power on

Press the button for two seconds to start. The machine begins at a safe default setting.

3

Set stroke depth

Rotate the handle grip to adjust needle depth between 2.2 and 3.3 mm across 6 discrete settings. No tools required.
