Key Features

Variable Output
4–12V range, adjustable in 0.5V steps with + and − controls. Startup default at 7V.
Autoclavable

Short-circuit protection

Setup & Use

Ready in three steps

1

Attach the FLY needle cartridge

The I-FLY is compatible exclusively with FLY needle cartridges. Assemble the needle on the pen before powering on.

2

Power on

Hold the button for two seconds to start. The machine launches at 7V by default. Tap once to stop and start during the session.

3

Adjust needle depth

Rotate the grip section to set needle depth. Numbered depth markings (2.2, 2.5, 2.8 mm visible on barrel) for precise, repeatable settings.

Note: take a 10-minute break from the device approximately every two hours of continuous use.
