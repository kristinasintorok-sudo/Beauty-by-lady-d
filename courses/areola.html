Curriculum

What you will learn

A highly specialised paramedical technique for creating natural-looking areola and nipple pigmentation for suitable clients following breast surgery, reconstruction or other changes to the breast area. Your 1:1 private training combines colour theory, realistic design, symmetry, skin assessment and hands-on live model practice.

  • Anatomy of the breast and post-surgical skin assessment
  • Colour theory and pigment selection for realistic areola recreation
  • 3D shading and texture techniques for depth and realism
  • Symmetry and placement — working from reference and client anatomy
  • Nipple projection illusion — creating a three-dimensional result
  • Scar tissue assessment and needle adaptation
  • Client consultation, consent and sensitivity protocols
  • Aftercare, healing management and touch-up planning
  • Clinic setup and professional practice guidance
  • Supervised live model practice
  • Clinita Italy protocol — full working method

Who this course is for

Areola micropigmentation is an advanced paramedical technique. This course requires active PMU or tattoo experience — working on clients with a needle is the foundation on which this training builds. It is not suitable for beginners.

It is also a natural next step for practitioners who have already completed Scar Camouflage or Inkless Needling training and want to extend into one of the most meaningful specialist services available.

  • PMU artists with active client experience
  • Tattoo artists looking to specialise in paramedical work
  • Practitioners who have completed Scar Camouflage or Inkless Needling training
  • Experienced paramedical practitioners ready to add this specialist service

This course is not for beginners or practitioners without prior experience working on skin with a needle. The client group is vulnerable — post-surgical clients require clinical sensitivity and technical precision that only comes with a solid professional foundation.

FAQ

Common questions

What experience do I need?

Active PMU or tattoo experience working on clients is required. Prior completion of Scar Camouflage or Inkless Needling training is an advantage but not mandatory — the core requirement is a solid, active professional foundation in working on skin with a needle.

Who are the clients for this treatment?

Post-mastectomy clients, those who have undergone breast reconstruction, and individuals experiencing changes to the breast area. It is a deeply meaningful treatment that can have a significant impact on confidence and wellbeing.

Is there a model practice session?

Yes — all courses include supervised live model practice. Tatiana will brief you on everything you need to arrange beforehand.

Can I take this alongside Scar Camouflage or Inkless Needling?

Yes — many practitioners complete multiple courses. Each course builds independently and the skills are highly complementary.

Yes. A deposit secures your place and the balance is due on the first day of training.
