Who this is for

Who this course is for

Scar camouflage is an advanced paramedical technique that requires precision, skin knowledge and clinical judgement. This course is for practitioners who already hold active experience in PMU or tattoo artistry — that foundation is essential for safe, consistent results on a vulnerable client population.

It is also a strong fit for practitioners who are already working in this area but want to move away from working by instinct — and instead follow a structured clinical protocol that produces consistent, defensible results.

  • PMU artists with active client experience
  • Tattoo artists looking to expand into paramedical services
  • Paramedical practitioners with a foundation in dermopigmentation
  • Experienced practitioners already offering scar treatments who want a proper protocol

This course is not suitable for beginners or practitioners without prior experience working on skin with a needle. The client group is vulnerable and the standard of care required demands a solid professional foundation.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior experience?

Yes. Active experience in PMU or tattoo artistry is required before taking this course. A working foundation in machine use and skin behaviour is essential for safe, consistent results on this client group.

Who are the clients for scar camouflage?

People with surgical scars, traumatic scarring, stretch marks and skin tone irregularities. It is a deeply meaningful treatment with growing awareness and demand.

What machine does the course use?

The Clinita I-FLY machine — the same machine used in the studio daily. It is included in the course price and is yours to keep after training.

Is a payment plan available?

Yes. A deposit secures your place and the balance is due on the first day of training.

Can I also take the Inkless Needling course?

Yes — many students complete both paramedical courses. The Clinita I-FLY machine is shared across both, so you only need it once.
