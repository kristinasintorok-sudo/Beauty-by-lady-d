FAQ

Common questions

Do I need prior experience?

No. This course is open to complete beginners, beauty professionals, aestheticians, nurses and PMU artists. We teach everything from theory to live model practice.

Who are the clients for scar camouflage?

People with surgical scars, traumatic scarring, stretch marks and skin tone irregularities. It is a deeply meaningful treatment with growing awareness and demand.

What machine does the course use?

The Clinita I-FLY machine — the same machine used in the studio daily. It is included in the course price and is yours to keep after training.

Is a payment plan available?

Yes. A deposit secures your place and the balance is due on the first day of training.

Can I also take the Inkless Needling course?

Yes — many students complete both paramedical courses. The Clinita I-FLY machine is shared across both, so you only need it once.
