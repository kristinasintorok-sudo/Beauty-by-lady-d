Curriculum

What you will learn

Saline removal is one of the most requested correction services among existing PMU clients — giving you the ability to address colour migration, outdated work and client regret. A highly valuable addition to any established PMU practice.

  • How saline removal works — osmosis process and pigment extraction
  • Skin science and wound healing in the context of removal
  • Assessing suitability — skin type, pigment depth and age of work
  • Technique protocol — machine settings, needle selection and pass depth
  • Managing expectations and predicting outcomes across sessions
  • Risks, contraindications and safety considerations
  • Aftercare, scabbing management and healing stages
  • Session planning — spacing, number of sessions and touch-up timing
  • Client consultation, consent and photographic record keeping
  • Supervised live model practice

FAQ

Common questions

What PMU experience do I need?

You should already be working on clients before taking this course. A solid understanding of machine use, skin and pigment is the foundation saline removal builds on.

How many sessions does saline removal take?

Results vary depending on pigment depth, colour and skin type. Multiple sessions spaced 6–8 weeks apart are typically required — this is covered in full during training.

Can saline removal be used on all PMU areas?

Yes — the technique applies to brows, lips and eyeliner. The course covers approach differences for each area.

Is there a model practice session?

Yes — all courses include supervised live model practice. Tatiana will brief you on everything you need to arrange beforehand.
